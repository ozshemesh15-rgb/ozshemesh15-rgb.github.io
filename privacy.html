Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the I'M BORED. iOS game uses and which optional services handle data when you choose to use them.

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Current release: Private TestFlight beta

1. Who operates the app

I'M BORED. is an independently developed iOS puzzle game. This policy covers the mobile game and the related support and privacy pages. For privacy-related questions during private beta testing, use TestFlight → I'M BORED. → Send Beta Feedback. A direct support email will be added before public launch.

2. Guest gameplay and local storage

You can play as a guest without creating an online account. Your progress, completed levels, lives, streaks, personal bests, settings and optional gameplay diagnostics are stored locally on your iPhone. These game records are not currently synced to an online account or sent to an advertising service by the game.

Updating the app normally preserves local data, but removing the app or clearing its data may permanently erase progress. Signing in does not currently enable cross-device save synchronization.

3. Optional account sign-in

If you choose to create or access an account, the app uses Firebase Authentication (provided by Google) and may offer email/password, Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In. Authentication providers may process the email address, unique account identifiers, display name or profile details made available by the provider, authentication tokens, and security-related logs as needed to authenticate you and protect accounts.

The game does not directly collect your payment card details, and it does not store your account password in readable form. Account sign-in is optional; guest play remains available. The app allows sign-out and an in-app account deletion request. Certain deletion actions may require recent authentication. Local game saves may remain on your device after online account deletion.

For additional details, see Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy.

4. Optional rewarded advertising

The game has an optional rewarded-ad feature. A player may choose to watch an available video ad in exchange for one heart; there are no mandatory video ads between levels or banner ads during play. A free heart-refill route remains available without watching an ad.

Current private TestFlight builds use Google-provided sample/test ads, not monetized live advertisements. The production AdMob identifiers have not been activated in the beta. A future public version may use live Google AdMob ads after app review and an update to this policy if needed.

Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers (depending on platform settings and permissions), approximate location derived from network information, ad delivery/diagnostics data, and privacy consent choices. Google and its advertising partners may use this information according to their policies. The game does not send your puzzle progress to AdMob for ad targeting.

Where required, the app checks advertising consent and presents privacy choices before requesting ads. You may be able to review or change applicable choices from the in-game menu when a privacy-options control is required. iOS App Tracking Transparency permissions, where applicable, are separate from Google consent choices. See Google Privacy and Google AdMob privacy information.

5. Optional Apple in-app purchases

The game may offer two optional purchases through Apple's StoreKit: (1) the permanent Banana Supporter Golden Frame cosmetic, and (2) a consumable five-heart refill available after you run out of hearts. The five-heart pack can be purchased again when needed. The game refills your heart meter to a maximum of five, and any purchased overflow hearts are saved on your device for subsequent failures. You may always choose to wait for free heart refills every 30 minutes, replay cleared puzzles or watch an optional rewarded ad when available.

Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, and we do not collect or store credit card numbers. The app verifies StoreKit transactions; locally stored transaction identifiers and pending-delivery records prevent duplicate heart grants. The permanent cosmetic can be restored through Apple. Consumable hearts are not restorable from your Apple purchase history after uninstall or moving devices; unused local hearts may be lost when app data is deleted. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox environment and do not charge real money.

6. Feedback, sharing, notifications and diagnostics

  • Sharing: Challenge codes and share cards are sent via the iOS share sheet only when you choose to share them.
  • Local notifications: Life-refill reminders are optional and require notification permission. They are scheduled on your device; no marketing push service is used by the game.
  • Private playtest diagnostics: Certain gameplay events and aggregate puzzle statistics may be stored locally to help you inspect the beta experience. They are not automatically uploaded by the game; you may choose to copy and share a report.
  • Support feedback: If you voluntarily send feedback through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may process the message and attached screenshots or diagnostics in accordance with Apple's policies.

7. Disclosure, retention and security

We do not sell players' locally stored puzzle progress. Personal information used for optional account sign-in, advertising consent/ads, or purchases may be processed by Firebase/Google, Apple and advertising technology providers where applicable. Those providers control their own processing and retention practices. Data stored only in the game remains until you remove or reset it, subject to the game's local data controls and iOS behavior. No online service is risk-free; we use established SDKs and platform security mechanisms for the features described above.

8. Choices and privacy rights

You may choose guest play, decline optional rewarded ads, manage supported advertising consent options, disable notification permission in iOS Settings, sign out, and request online account deletion from within the game. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, object to or restrict certain processing of personal information. For account-related requests during the beta, start with the app's account controls; for other requests, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback and state that the request concerns privacy. We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request.

9. Children and age suitability

The game is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information. The final App Store age rating and any additional age-related advertising safeguards will be set before public distribution. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an account, contact us through the available beta support channel.

10. International processing and updates

Google, Apple and their service providers may process data in other countries as described in their respective notices. We may update this policy to reflect new features, providers or legal requirements. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. Any materially different data use in a public release will be described before it is enabled.

Beta status: This policy describes the game's current private testing setup. Public monetization, public App Store disclosures, complete direct developer contact information and final age-related settings require additional review before a public launch.
