🍌 Support

The banana has questions too. This is the official support page for I'M BORED. on iPhone.

Need help or want to report a bug?

Email ozshemeshart@gmail.com with the subject I'M BORED Support. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, puzzle level and a screenshot if relevant. For privacy requests, use the same address with the subject I'M BORED Privacy. You can also use Apple’s TestFlight feedback feature while testing.

Don't lose your progress

Your game saves currently remain on your iPhone. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot: uninstalling can erase locally stored progress and unused heart packs. Update using TestFlight or the App Store instead.

Questions about ads or purchases?

Rewarded ads are optional. A five-heart refill can be earned by completing TWO rewarded ads; the first ad alone gives no hearts, and closing either ad before it earns Google's reward does not count. Your 1/2 progress is retained locally. Private TestFlight builds use Google's sample ads and generate no advertising revenue; when enabled in the public app, rewarded ads use Google's own advertising service and applicable consent controls. Hearts refill free every 30 minutes; cleared levels can be replayed free. When the heart meter is empty, an optional Apple consumable purchase refills up to five hearts, banking any unused purchased overflow locally on the same device. A separate one-time Banana Supporter cosmetic is also optional. Apple shows the current local price before any purchase. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox and do not charge real money; purchases made in the public App Store app use Apple's real payments and localized pricing. Consumable hearts cannot be restored on another device or after deleting the app. The Golden Frame cosmetic can be restored from Apple purchases.
